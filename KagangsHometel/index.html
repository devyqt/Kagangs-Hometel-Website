About Kagang’s Hometel

Established in 2022, Kagang’s Hometel is perfectly located at the center of Las Navas’ commercial zone. The hometel is just steps away from local cafés, transport terminals, a remittance center, Rural Health Unit (RHU), the new public market, and grocery stores. Whether you’re visiting for business or leisure, Kagang’s Hometel offers the convenience of the town with the warmth of a home.

Mission

At Kagang’s Hometel, we provide warm and comfortable stays that reflect the hospitality of Las Navas.

Vision

To be the top local hotel in Las Navas—known for heartfelt service, home-like comfort, and strong community ties.

Hotel Reminders

  • All guests must present a valid ID upon check-in.
  • The hotel strictly enforces a no-smoking policy. A Php 5,000 fine will be charged for any violation.
  • The hotel does not accommodate short-time stays.
  • The management treats the hotel as their home. Guests are encouraged to show respect to the property, staff, and fellow guests.
  • The hotel is intended as a “pahuwayan” (resting place). We do not allow liquor inside rooms, loud gatherings, parties, or any activity that may disturb other guests.

Kagang’s Hometel, established in 2022, is a thoughtfully located accommodation in the heart of Las Navas’ commercial district. Surrounded by cafés, transport hubs, health services, and markets, it offers travelers both convenience and comfort — a welcoming stay whether for business or leisure.
